## 4. European & UK ATS Selectors (`enrichment/detail.py`)

European and UK employers frequently use EU-headquartered ATS platforms whose DOM structures are not covered by `v0.3.0`'s US-centric CSS selector list:
- **Personio** (`*.jobs.personio.de`, `*.jobs.personio.com`): `.job-detail-description`, `a.career-btn-apply`
- **Teamtailor**: `.body-text`, `[data-controller="career--apply-button"] a`
- **Pinpoint HQ** (`*.pinpointhq.com`): `.rt-text`, `a[href*="/applications/new"]`
- **SmartRecruiters** (`jobs.smartrecruiters.com`): `[itemprop="description"]`, `a#st-apply`
- **Workable** (`apply.workable.com`): `[data-ui="job-description"]`, `a[data-ui="overview-apply-now"]`
- **Recruitee**: `.description`, `a[href*="/c/new"]`

Adding these selectors to `DESCRIPTION_SELECTORS` and `APPLY_SELECTORS` in `enrichment/detail.py` keeps extraction in Tier 1/2 (zero LLM tokens, instant speed).
